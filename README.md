### 🚀 About Me
I'm a Software Engineer who loves solving complex problems across the entire stack. Whether it's building robust backends in **.NET**, crafting type-safe frontends with **TypeScript**, or leveraging **Python** for automation and data, I focus on writing clean, maintainable code. I also care about quality end to end, with hands-on **General QA** experience: test design, manual and automated testing, and bug hunting.
